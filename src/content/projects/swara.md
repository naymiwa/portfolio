**SWARA** (Sky Watch with Audio & Real-time Access) is a web platform built during the **NASA Hackathon 2026**, turning NASA's SPHEREx infrared sky survey data into an accessible exploration tool. It lets users search celestial objects, compare time-series sky images from 2010–2026, inspect spectral data, and even "listen" to the sky through data sonification.
